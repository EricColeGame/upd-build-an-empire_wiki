## What the "[UPD]" Tag Actually Means on Roblox

Roblox hosts millions of experiences, and developers rely on title conventions to distinguish one version of a listing from another. The bracketed **[UPD]** prefix is one of those conventions — it tells players that the page they're looking at represents the current, maintained build rather than an abandoned prototype or an old re-upload.

That distinction matters when you're hunting for release information. A fresh tag, a rewritten description, or a new thumbnail is often the earliest public hint that something changed. The tag itself is set by the developer, though, so it isn't a formal version number you can cite. Treat it as a strong signal, not a stamped certificate.

| Title tag | What it usually signals | How much to trust it |
|---|---|---|
| [UPD] | The listing is the current, updated build | High — for "this game is maintained" |
| [BETA] | Content is still in development | Medium — features may change |
| [NEW] | Recently launched or refreshed | Medium — verify with the description |
