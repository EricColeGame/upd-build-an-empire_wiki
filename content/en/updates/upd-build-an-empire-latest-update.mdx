## Army, Flags, and Map Control

Combat is where economies are tested. You can build the richest empire on the server and still lose the map if your army is a single unit type thrown at a fortified position.

| Unit | Role | Best used for |
|---|---|---|
| Soldiers | Core damage | Early fights and flag pushes |
| Giants | Frontline tanks | Absorbing damage in large battles |
| Medics | Sustain and healing | Keeping long pushes alive |
| Siege weapons | Structure damage | Breaking fortified positions |

A practical rule from player experience: never start a fight without Medics, and never start a fight you cannot reinforce. Flag captures are the real objective, not kills. An empire that holds flags generates more income, which funds a bigger army, which holds more flags. That feedback loop is the entire point of the military layer.

The [UPD] Build an Empire latest update reinforces that loop rather than replacing it. If you are returning after a break, spend your first session scouting instead of attacking. Identify which nearby empires are inactive, which flags are lightly defended, and where your army composition has an obvious gap.

## FAQ

**What is the [UPD] Build an Empire latest update actually about?**
It is a refresh of the core empire-building loop. You still start with a small piece of land, grow farms, mines, houses, and castles, sell resources, research upgrades, and build an army of soldiers, Giants, Medics, and siege weapons to capture flags. The update cycle keeps that loop fresh rather than replacing it.

**Do I need Premium to progress?**
No. Premium adds a five percent sell value bonus, which is helpful but not required. Joining the game's group for a ten percent bonus is free and arguably the better first move for new players.

**Does my empire keep running when I log off?**
Yes. Offline progress is a core feature, meaning your buildings continue producing while you are away. Log back in, collect, and reinvest rather than letting resources sit idle.

**What should I build first as a new player?**
Farms and a mine, then enough houses to staff them. Get a reliable sell rhythm going before you spend on castles or military units. Research that boosts production or sell value should come next, followed by expansion and a balanced army.

**How do I win fights against stronger empires?**
Do not fight them head-on. Scout for lightly defended flags, bring Medics for sustain, and use Giants to absorb damage while your soldiers and siege weapons do the work. Map control compounds into income, which eventually closes the power gap.
