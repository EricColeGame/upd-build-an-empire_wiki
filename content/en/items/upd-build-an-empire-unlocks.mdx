## The Three Unlock Tracks You're Actually Managing

Most players treat progression as one long grind. In practice, three separate tracks run in parallel, and each one gates something different. Knowing which track you are pushing — and why — keeps you from pouring resources into the wrong place.

| Track | What it unlocks | How you push it forward | Early priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research | Stronger buildings and passive bonuses | Spend produced resources on research projects | Highest — it multiplies everything else |
| Empire Level | Higher-tier content and overall prestige | Build structures, grow population, expand your plot | Medium — rises naturally as you build |
| Military | Soldiers, Giants, Medics, and siege weapons | Build an army, fight other empires, capture flags | Situational — needed for map control |

Here is the practical takeaway from that table:

- **Research compounds.** A bonus you unlock early pays out on every single sale you make afterward.
- **Empire Level is a byproduct.** If you are building and expanding steadily, it climbs on its own. Chasing it directly is usually slower.
- **Military is a switch, not a slope.** You do not need an army until you want map control. Before that, every coin spent on soldiers is a coin not compounding in your economy.

Community reports from players grinding the current build suggest the same pattern: the people who snowball are the ones who treat combat as a late decision rather than an early identity.
