Your empire in Roblox's [UPD] Build an Empire! can grow from a single plot of land into a sprawling kingdom — but none of it survives without an army. Getting your [UPD] Build an Empire units right is the biggest decision you'll make after your first farm, because every raid, flag capture, and counterattack runs through your roster. This guide breaks down each unit type, how to unlock them, and the army compositions that actually hold territory.

## Why Units Make or Break Your Empire

In most Roblox tycoon-style empire games, the economy is the easy part. Farms, mines, and houses generate income whether you're paying attention or not — and in [UPD] Build an Empire!, your empire keeps producing even while you're offline. The hard part is protecting all of it.

Units are what convert resources into staying power. Without them, a wealthy empire is just a well-stocked target. With them, you can capture flags, push other players off contested ground, and control the map instead of reacting to whoever attacks you first.

There's a second reason the roster matters: research. Upgrades in [UPD] Build an Empire! unlock stronger buildings and bonuses, and many of those bonuses only pay off if you have the army to use them. A production bonus on a base nobody can defend is wasted money.

Finally, unit choice affects your economy indirectly. Every coin you sink into soldiers, Giants, Medics, and siege weapons is a coin you didn't put into a mine. That trade-off is the core loop of the game, and understanding each unit's role is how you stop guessing at it.

## The [UPD] Build an Empire Units Roster at a Glance

The game's official description highlights four army families: soldiers, Giants, Medics, and siege weapons. Each one fills a distinct slot, and no single unit type wins a fight alone.

| Unit | Battlefield Role | What It Does Well | Where It Struggles |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soldiers | Core infantry | Fast to field, effective in numbers, flexible on offense and defense | Individually fragile; melt against Giants without support |
| Giants | Frontline tank | Absorb damage, lead pushes, break through defensive lines | Slow to move; need Soldiers behind them to finish fights |
| Medics | Support | Keep squads alive through longer engagements and sieges | Almost no offensive output; useless without a frontline |
| Siege Weapons | Structure breaker | Pressure fortifications and support flag captures | Vulnerable when unescorted; poor at defending a base |

The takeaway from this table is simple: your [UPD] Build an Empire units should never be a single-unit stack. Four unit types mean four jobs, and the players who climb fastest are the ones who treat the roster like a team rather than a shopping list.

### A quick note on how these roles interact

Community reports from Roblox clip hubs consistently show the same pattern in fights: Giants go in first, Soldiers follow, Medics sit behind the line, and siege weapons stay back until the defenders are distracted. Break that order and you lose units you didn't need to lose.

## How to Unlock and Train Units

You don't start with a barracks full of Giants. Progression in [UPD] Build an Empire! is gated behind economy, research, and Empire Level. Here's the order that works.

| Step | Action | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build farms, mines, and houses | Resource production is the foundation of every unit you'll ever train |
| 2 | Sell produced resources | Converts raw output into the currency you spend on buildings and troops |
| 3 | Research upgrades | Unlocks stronger buildings, unit tiers, and passive bonuses |
| 4 | Construct military buildings | These are the structures that actually produce your army |
| 5 | Raise your Empire Level | Higher levels gate the better unit tiers and bigger building options |
| 6 | Train a balanced first squad | Avoid dumping everything into one unit type you can't support |

Two multipliers are worth knowing about because they speed up this whole process. Premium membership grants a +5% sell value bonus, and joining the official group adds +10% sell value. Stacked together, that's a meaningful head start on funding your first real army — and since your empire works offline, that bonus keeps compounding while you're away.

### Balancing Economy and Military Spending

Most players either over-build defense and stall, or over-build army and go broke. A rough framework — treat this as a starting point, not a rule — looks like this:

| Empire Stage | Economy Focus | Military Focus | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | Heavy — farms and mines first | Minimal — a token defensive squad | You need income before you need an army |
| Mid Game | Moderate — upgrade existing producers | Growing — first Giants and Medics | Start contesting nearby flags |
| Late Game | Maintenance — research bonuses do the work | Heavy — full combined-arms squads | Territory now pays for itself |

The signal to shift from economy to military is simple: when you can't spend your income fast enough, you've built enough economy.

## Army Composition: Which Units to Train First

Composition is where the [UPD] Build an Empire units roster stops being a list and starts being a strategy. The table below shows a progression that mirrors how most successful players build.

| Stage | Suggested Core | Support | Primary Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early | Soldiers | None or light | Defend farms and claim easy nearby flags |
| Mid | Soldiers plus your first Giant | One or two Medics | Push into contested ground and hold it |
| Late | Giants with Soldier backup | Medics and siege weapons | Break fortified positions and control the map |

A few practical rules sit underneath that table:

- **Never send Giants alone.** They soak damage, they don't finish fights. Pair them with Soldiers who can actually clear defenders.
- **One Medic per two or three frontliners** is a reasonable ratio to aim for in longer fights.
- **Keep siege weapons in the back.** They're your answer to walls and defensive structures, not your answer to enemy troops.
- **Rebuild before you expand.** Losing a full squad to a counterattack costs more than the flag was worth.

Player experience suggests that the biggest single mistake is over-investing in one unit type early. A stack of Soldiers with no Giants gets shredded by a tank-led push, and a stack of Giants with no Soldiers can't finish anything.

## Combat, Flags, and Map Control

Fighting in [UPD] Build an Empire! is objective-based, not just kill-based. Flags and map control are the actual win conditions, which means the right unit for the job depends entirely on what you're trying to do.

| Objective | Units to Lean On | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Defend your base | Soldiers plus Medics | Fast response times and sustain beat raw durability on home ground |
| Capture a flag | Giants plus Soldiers | Giants absorb the first wave so Soldiers can swarm the point |
| Break a fortified position | Siege weapons with an escort | Structures need dedicated damage, not more infantry |
| Hold a captured flag | Mixed squad with Medics | Sustain matters more than burst when you're the one being attacked |

A simple engagement checklist that works at almost every Empire Level:

1. Scout the target before committing anything.
2. Send Giants in first to eat the initial damage.
3. Follow with Soldiers to clear defenders.
4. Keep Medics positioned behind the frontline, not in it.
5. Bring siege weapons only once the defenders are engaged.

If you follow that order and still lose, the problem is usually numbers or upgrades — not composition.

## FAQ

**What unit types are available in [UPD] Build an Empire?**
The army roster includes soldiers, Giants, Medics, and siege weapons, as listed in the game's official description. Soldiers are your core infantry, Giants act as damage-absorbing tanks, Medics keep squads alive, and siege weapons are built for breaking fortifications.

**How do I unlock stronger [UPD] Build an Empire units?**
Stronger units come from progression, not shortcuts. Build your economy first, sell resources for income, invest in research upgrades, construct the military buildings that produce troops, and keep raising your Empire Level — higher levels gate the better tiers.

**What's the best army composition for a new player?**
Start with Soldiers only while you establish income. Add your first Giant and a Medic or two once you're contesting flags regularly, then introduce siege weapons when you're attacking fortified bases. A balanced squad beats a specialized one almost every time.

**Do my units and empire keep working while I'm offline?**
Yes. [UPD] Build an Empire! is designed so your empire continues producing while you're away, which means your resource stockpile — and therefore your ability to fund the next batch of units — grows even when you're not logged in.

**Is there a way to earn money faster for units?**
Two bonuses help: Premium membership adds +5% to sell value, and joining the official group adds +10%. Since both apply to everything you sell, they effectively reduce the cost of every unit you train.
